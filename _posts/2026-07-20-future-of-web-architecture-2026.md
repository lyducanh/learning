Web aesthetics in 2026 are defined by **vibrant depth, subtle micro-interactions, dark glassmorphism**, and hyper-fast initial page loads.

## Essential Design Aesthetics

- **Curated Color Palettes**: Ditching standard plain colors for HSL tuned deep space dark hues (`#07090e`) with electric cyan (`#00f2fe`) and violet (`#7928ca`) accents.
- **Glassmorphic Depth**: Leveraging `backdrop-filter: blur()` combined with subtle multi-layer radial light sources.
- **Typography Excellence**: Pairing geometric display headers (`Outfit`) with high-readability body text (`Inter`).
